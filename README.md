# Bitcoin Market Sentiment vs Trader Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between Bitcoin market sentiment and trader performance using Hyperliquid historical trading data and the Bitcoin Fear & Greed Index.

The objective is to understand how market sentiment affects trader profitability, win rates, trading volume, fees, and overall trading behavior.

---

## Datasets

### Hyperliquid Historical Trader Data

Contains:

- Account
- Coin
- Execution Price
- Size USD
- Side
- Closed PnL
- Fee
- Timestamp

### Bitcoin Fear & Greed Index

Contains:

- Date
- Classification
- Fear & Greed Value

## Key Findings

### Profitability

Extreme Greed generated the highest average trader profitability.

### Win Rate

The highest win rate was observed during Extreme Greed periods.

### Trading Volume

Fear periods generated the highest trading volume.

### Trading Fees

Fear periods showed the highest average trading fees.

### Trader Performance

A small number of traders generated a significant share of total profits.

### Asset Performance

Trading activity and profitability varied significantly across cryptocurrencies.

---

## Conclusion

Market sentiment has a measurable impact on trader behavior and trading outcomes. Extreme Greed periods were associated with higher profitability and win rates, while Fear periods produced increased market activity and trading costs.

These findings suggest that sentiment indicators can be useful when developing trading strategies and risk management frameworks.
